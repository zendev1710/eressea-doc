# Weapons

This page lists combat-relevant properties of individual weapons.  
For the aggregate table, see [this table][weapons-summary-table].

## Melee weapons

Every melee weapon requires having learned the [melee skill][skill-melee-id].  

Melee weapons deal the same damage on foot and while mounted.

### Catapult

A catapult requires having learned the [catapult skill][skill-catapult-id].  

Catapults only fire **in the first round** of combat.  

They hit **6** targets.  

*Characteristics of a catapult in combat.*

| Damage                | 3d10+5 |
| Average damage        |   21.5 |
| Attack bonus/penalty  |     -4 |

| Total bonus/penalty   |     -4 |

### Crossbow

A crossbow requires having learned the [bow skill][skill-crossbow-id].  

Crossbows only fire in the 1st and 4th rounds of combat.  

A crossbow hit **halves the armour protection** of the opponent.
