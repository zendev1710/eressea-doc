# Armed

A unit is considered armed and ready for fight if it possesses a [[skills#combat-skills|combat skill]] and at least one weapon of the corresponding type.

!!! note "Important"
    A unit **must be armed and ready for fight** to be able to:

    - [[cmd-guard|guard]] a region
    - [[skills-list#taxation|collect taxes]]

[[monsters|Monsters]] and other units with the [[skills-list#unarmed-combat|unarmed combat]] skill are considered armed even when they are not carrying a weapon.  
They are, in fact, armed with teeth and claws (or tentacles...).
