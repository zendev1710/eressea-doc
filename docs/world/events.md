## Peasants hike

Peasants are able to move from region to region.  
Migrating peasants do not work during the week of the migration, but still consume silver (in the target region).

Peasants emigrate for different reasons.

### Fleeing from monsters

Peasants flee from [[monsters]].  

If monsters appear in a region, they drive away some peasants every week.  
The number of peasants displaced depends on the type and number of monsters.  

Monsters summoned by mages can also drive away peasants.  

### Overpopulation

Peasants are emigrating because of overpopulation.  
If there are more Peasants than [jobs][cmd-work] in a region, some of the unemployed emigrate to neighboring regions.  

### Poverty

Peasants emigrate also because of poverty.
