# Armed

A unit is considered armed and ready for fight if it possesses a [combat skill] and at least one weapon of the corresponding type.

!!! note "Important"

    A unit **must be armed and ready for fight** to be able to:

    - [[cmd-guard|guard]] a region
    - [collect taxes](./skills-list.md#taxation)

[[monsters|Monsters]] and other units with the [Unarmed Combat] skill are considered armed even when they are not carrying a weapon.  
They are, in fact, armed with teeth and claws (or tentacles...).
