# RESEARCH

*[[orders#short-and-long-orders|long order]].*  

**`RESEARCH HERBS`**  

With this order the unit tries to find out which [[herbs|herb]] grows in a region and approximately how much of it is present.  

To do this, the unit must have at least [[skills-list#herbalism|herbalism]] **T7**.
